# Independent progress-continuation and moving-stop review

Decision: **PASS for the discrete progress-speed continuation helper and this focused moving-stop replay envelope. Phase 5 acceptance remains pending.** Position/collision stopping from arbitrary states, real-time execution and hardware safety are not established.

The old shared greedy helper was independently linked and executed against the preserved v19 library. Starting r=.001, previous b=-.04, dt=.004, speed cap.2, acceleration cap.5 and jerk cap5, it chose b=-.06,-.08,-.10, leaving r=.00004 and no feasible next acceleration. An independent exact rational oracle gives the viable sequence -.06,-.0775,-.0575,-.0375,-.0175,0, ending at zero speed and acceleration without violating any declared bound. Source and old counterexample remain in `results/phase5-reference/progress_stop_continuation_counterexample.json` and `results/phase5-early-review/root-progress-stop-counterexample`.

The repaired helper intersects the selected input with both exact discrete speed-continuation cones and preserves the existing per-step acceleration, jerk and progress-position bounds. The stationary-point implementation was independently checked against an all-integer-prefix oracle on five analytic cases and 1,000 sampled states: 955 feasible, 45 rejected, zero ambiguous boundary samples; maximum bound discrepancy 6.495e-15. Every admitted sample permits a bounded-jerk recovery to zero acceleration while speed stays inside its bounds. This does not mean every sampled case stops at zero speed or proves universal position/collision viability. Artifacts: `root-progress-continuation-fixed`.
